# Case 017 reels: five 25-second teasers

Format: 9:16, 1080×1920, 30 fps, **25.0 s = 750 frames**. One voice, recorded last.
Spoiler rule: never show or name the killer, never mention the clock, never connect anyone to the fraud. Each reel ends on a question.

## Style sheet (matches the reference reel)

- **Art:** flat 2D vector, no outlines, soft vignette, light film grain. Characters are side-profile cut-outs. They slide, turn their heads, swing their limbs and stretch; no lip sync and almost no facial animation. Use hands, feet, silhouettes and objects for the drama.
- **Colour grades, one per scene:**
  - Night: navy `#0f1a24`.
  - Lamp: amber `#c9a66b` glow.
  - Morning: grey-blue mist.
  - Paper: cream `#f1e9d8`.
  - Accent: the caption orange only.
- **Transitions:** cross-dissolves of 8–12 frames, slow push-ins of 2–4%, and parallax mist. At most one hard cut per reel, used for the shock beat.
- **Captions:**
  - Bold sans in the Poppins style, white, 2–4 words per chunk.
  - The spoken word turns orange (karaoke style).
  - Placed in the top third, or the bottom third when the action is at the top. Never over a face or a document's key line.
- **Documents on screen:** keep to one or two big lines (a watch screen, a camera timestamp, a stamped word). Nothing smaller than a caption.
- **Sound:** low drone, clock tick, rain or wind. Drop to near silence on the shock line.
- **The cast stays faceless when it matters:**
  - Vikram: silver hair, moustache, grey shawl.
  - The doctor: rimless glasses, black bag.
  - The housekeeper: in a saree.
  - Anyone else in the house is a shadow, hands or feet only.

### End card (identical in all five, 20.6 s → 25.0 s, frames 618–750)
- Hollow Pines at night, wide and blurred, as in the reference's "The End" card. A small manila folder with T.B.B fades in at the centre.
- 20.8–22.0 (f624–660): **"That's what we do here"**
- 22.0–23.4 (f660–702): **"at The Blackwood Bureau."**
- 23.4–25.0 (f702–750): under it, small and cream: **theblackwoodbureau.com** (no voice; hold).

### Timing method
- The pace is about 2.5 words a second, like the reference.
- Times below are targets. After recording the voice, run forced alignment (for example WhisperX or Aeneas) and slide each caption chunk to its words. Scene cuts are tied to chunk starts, so they move with the voice.
- Numbers are written as digits on screen but spelled out in the voice script ("nine forty-four").
- Chunk boundaries sit on natural breaths, so the voice actor should pause where the chunks break.

## Release order and hooks

1. Part 1, The Watch: the strongest hook, a dead man's watch.
2. Part 4, The Voice: a ghostly, shareable mystery.
3. Part 2, The Gate Camera: "who's lying" makes people argue in the comments.
4. Part 3, The Second Needle: the method.
5. Part 5, The Sealed Letter: the motive, the last push to play.
