# Notes for Claude

## Named art styles
- **"Low poly" / "create in low poly"**: read `styles/low-poly/STYLE.md` first. It describes the reference art style (faceted vector-painting illustrations, cinematic, AI image-to-video motion, 25fps, hard cuts every ~2s); `dino-supermarket/` is only a starting toolkit.
- **"Zomato style" / "create on this" with a photo background**: read `styles/flat-on-photo/STYLE.md` first. The user's photo stays untouched as the background; flat halftone-and-ink illustrated props (flat-lay on flat tables) and kinetic words animate on top, slow pace, 25fps, 1080×1920. Approved version: `reel-zomato-mat/`.
