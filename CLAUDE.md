# Notes for Claude

## Named art styles
The approved styles are **low poly**, **mat** and **flat cutouts**; **PS2** is the newest (first build done). Zomato style is the earlier flat-on-photo version that mat replaced, kept for reference.

- **"Low poly" / "create in low poly"**: read `styles/low-poly/STYLE.md` first. It describes the reference art style (faceted vector-painting illustrations, cinematic, AI image-to-video motion, 25fps, hard cuts every ~2s); `dino-supermarket/` is only a starting toolkit.
- **"Zomato style" / "create on this" with a photo background**: read `styles/flat-on-photo/STYLE.md` first. The user's photo stays untouched as the background; flat halftone-and-ink illustrated props (flat-lay on flat tables) and kinetic words animate on top, slow pace, 25fps, 1080×1920. Approved version: `reel-zomato-mat/`.
- **"Mat style" / "create in mat style"**: read `styles/mat/STYLE.md` first. The Zomato video format (user's flat table photo untouched as background, kinetic words) with low-poly, high-detail Three.js items seen top-down; text in Poppins Bold, no outline, very slight soft shadow; 1080×1920, video 25fps, item motion stepped at 12.5fps. Reference build: `ramen-lowpoly/`.
- **"Flat cutouts" / "create in flat cutouts"**: read `styles/flat-cutouts/STYLE.md` first. Clean, minimal flat-vector storybook (Mae Nak reference): no outlines, soft radial glows, gradient skies, water reflections, fog; cutout-puppet characters rotating at joints with smooth 30fps motion; slow camera; Poppins captions with the spoken word highlighted orange; dissolves between scenes. 1080×1920, 30fps. Approved build: `sirens-cutouts/`.
- **"PS2" / "create in PS2"**: read `styles/ps2/STYLE.md` first. Low-res smooth low-poly 3D (faceless mannequin characters, big readable costumes), smeared with a Kuwahara oil-paint filter and softly upscaled, so it looks unclear and dreamlike; bright sun, fog, drifting particles, handheld camera; motion stepped at 12.5fps in a 25fps video. First build: `ps2-wizard/`.
