# Flat Cutouts: style reference

When the user says **"create in flat cutouts"** (or "flat cutout style"), this is what they mean.
**Reference:** the user's video "Mae Nak", a Folk Horror Tales episode (Tale No. 3). It runs 92s at 720×1280 and 30fps with voiceover. I studied it frame by frame: about 25 scenes, character motion checked at 30fps.
**Overall feel:** **clean, minimal, moody storybook animation.** Flat vector shapes, soft atmospheric light, puppet-like cutout characters, gentle camera motion and karaoke captions.

---

## 1. Format and pacing
- **Size and frame rate:** 9:16, rendered at 1080×1920 (the reference is 720×1280), **30fps**. Motion is **smooth on every frame**: no stepping, no held drawings.
- **Narration-led:** second person ("You are Mak…"), told calmly. Each scene holds **2–5s**, as long as its narration line.
- **Transitions:** soft **cross-dissolves** (about 0.4–0.8s), fades through black, and **fog wipes** (a white mist layer swells in, then clears onto the next scene).
- **Hard cuts:** occasional, only for shocks (the long arm, the ghost face).
- **Structure:**
  - A cold-open hook first.
  - A **title card** over the first wide shot: "FOLK HORROR TALES" in small spaced caps between thin rules, a big serif title ("MAE NAK", Cinzel/Trajan-like), and "– Tale No. 3 –" in italic serif. All of it is gold-cream at low opacity and fades in.
  - The story in short beats.
  - An end card: "The End" in italic serif, "MORE TALES SOON" in spaced caps, and a tiny "follow for the next tale".

## 2. Visual language
- **Pure flat vector shapes:** filled polygons, rectangles and circles, with **no outlines and no texture**. Detail comes from simple repeated shapes:
  - Wall panels as a grid of rectangles.
  - Thatch roofs as fan lines radiating from the peak.
  - Floor planks as horizontal lines.
  - Grass as small "v" ticks.
  - Water as thin horizontal streak lines.
- **Soft light is the main effect.** Use large **radial gradient glows** for lamps, windows, candles, the moon and doorways, plus **light beams** (soft vertical rectangles of light from a doorway, a jar or the water surface). Glows are additive and very soft.
- **Atmosphere:**
  - A full-frame **vertical gradient sky** for each scene.
  - **Haze bands** (soft, wide, low-opacity gradients) where land meets water.
  - **Fog** layers made of big blurred white ellipses.
  - A strong **vignette** that darkens the edges and especially the bottom.
- **Water:** a darker band below the horizon. It holds a **mirrored reflection** of the scene (flipped, about 25–35% opacity, darkened, slightly blurred) and a few thin horizontal ripple lines that slide slowly.
- **Depth** is built from 3–5 flat layers: far silhouettes (palms, distant village rooftops), mid scene, foreground. Each layer is darker or hazier with distance, and moves at its own speed in parallax pans.
- **Minimal composition:**
  - One clear subject per shot and lots of empty, quiet space.
  - Horizons are usually low or in the middle.
  - Close-ups for emotion and horror beats: feet beside a bush, eyes in the leaves, a giant ghost face over the village.

## 4. Characters (cutout puppets)
- **Construction:** rigid flat parts that rotate at joints, like paper puppets.
  - **Head:** a circle-ish skin shape seen in **profile**, with a dark **hair cap** shape (bun, crop, or long ghost hair). The eye is a small white almond with a dark dot. The mouth is a tiny line or dark oval. The ear is a small skin circle.
  - **Body:** a tapered tunic shape (shirt or blouse), with a **sash** or shawl as a separate angled band.
  - **Limbs:** long rounded tubes, upper and lower segments with round joints. **Hands are small circles.** Feet are small rounded wedges, often bare.
  - **Clothing:** solid colours (navy shirt `#2e3f78`, maroon trousers `#7a2a2a`, red and gold sash, a striped sarong in purple/blue with thin lines).
  - **Proportions:** slightly tall and slender, with a head about 1/6.5 of the body.
- **No outlines, minimal shading.** At most one slightly darker tone for the far-side limbs and a soft contact shadow under the feet.
- **Motion (checked at 30fps):** smooth rotation of each part every frame. The joint angles follow sine curves:
  - **Run cycle:** about 0.5s per stride. Arms and legs swing in opposition, the body leans forward, and there's a small body bob.
  - **Paddling:** the oar sweeps on a loop, the torso twists slightly, and the boat bobs on the water.
  - **Idle:** gentle breathing (torso scale ±1%) and occasional blinks.
- **Signature horror move:** **one limb stretches impossibly**. The arm extends as a growing tube down through the floorboards. It stays smooth and slow, no gore.
- **Ghost:** the same puppet, but with pale skin, long black hair and a wide dark mouth when screaming. It appears through dissolves and fades. A **giant translucent face** can float in the sky over the scene.

## 5. Camera
- **Slow, constant motion:** gentle pans across wide scenes, a slow push-in on tension, and a slight drift on held shots.
- **Easing:** in-out, never snappy.
- **Parallax:** layers move at different speeds during pans.
- **Occasional tracking:** the camera follows a running character from the side while the background scrolls.

## 6. Captions (key part of the look)
- **Font:** **Poppins SemiBold or Bold**, sentence case, white `#ffffff`, about 4.5% of the frame height.
- **Placement:** centred, at about **18–20% from the top** (sometimes at about 62–70%, kept clear of the subject).
- **Chunks:** 1–5 words at a time, one phrase per chunk.
- **Karaoke highlight:** the word being spoken is **orange `#f2a65a`**, and the rest stay white. The highlight moves word by word in time with the voiceover.
- **Styling:** no box and no outline, only a very soft dark shadow for legibility.
- **Animation:** chunks swap with a quick soft fade (about 0.15s). There's no bounce.
- **Cue words:** quotes for dialogue ("Mak? Where are you going?"), and ellipses and full stops for dread ("run.", "empty.").

## 7. Sound (in the reference)
Calm narrator, a low ambient drone and soft night sounds (water, insects), with small stings on horror beats. **Our videos:** the user records the voiceover, so captions are timed to it. Until then, use placeholder timing.

## 8. How to build it (code plan)
- **Canvas2D**, deterministic `window.render(t)`, 1080×1920 at 30fps, rendered with the lemo-opuscar `video.mjs --fps 30`.
- **Scenes:** each scene is a function `(ctx, localT)` drawing its flat layers back to front:
  - sky gradient
  - far silhouettes
  - glows (`radialGradient`, `globalCompositeOperation='lighter'` or `screen`)
  - mid layer
  - characters
  - water reflection (draw the scene to an offscreen canvas, flip it, use alpha .3 and a 2px blur)
  - fog and haze
  - vignette
- **Characters:** a puppet rig built from nested `ctx.save/translate/rotate`, with parts as filled paths. Poses are joint angles driven by smooth functions of time.
- **Transitions:** render both scenes and crossfade with alpha. A fog wipe is a growing blurred white layer.
- **Text:** Poppins from `ramen-lowpoly/fonts/`, loaded with `FontFace`, using the karaoke highlight as above.
- **Keep it clean:** no grain, no outlines, no clutter. When in doubt, remove detail and add soft light.

## 9. Approved build: `sirens-cutouts/` (10s, Sirens, Tale No. 4)
The user approved this build. **Copy it as the starting point** for new flat-cutouts videos.
It's a single `index.html` with Canvas2D, about 230 lines, and renders in about 40s for 10s of video.

### Shot plan used (a good template for a 10s tale)
| Time | Shot | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3.4s | Wide establishing shot: moonlit sea, the hero rows a lantern boat toward a far rock, slow pan. Title card fades in and out. | Set the mood and the series branding |
| 3.4–7s | Soft 0.55s dissolve to a medium shot: the threat (siren on a rock, pale glow, swaying tail, song rings drifting to the boat). The hero slows, stops and looks up. Slow 5% push-in. | Reveal |
| 7–10s | Quick 0.25s dissolve to a close shot: hull at the waterline, a pale face rising through the lantern's beam, an **arm stretching** toward the boat. Fade to black over the last 0.55s. | Horror beat and the stretched-limb signature |

### Helpers (all in `index.html`)
- **`sky(top, mid, horizon, y)`:** a 3-stop vertical gradient. `stars(t)`: seeded dots with slow twinkle. `moon(x, y, r)`: a disc with two faint craters plus a `glow` of 6× the radius.
- **`glow(x, y, r, 'r,g,b', a)`:** a radial gradient with `lighter` compositing, stops at 0, 0.35 (×0.45) and 1. This is the core light effect, used for lamps, the moon and the siren aura.
- **`water(horizon, a, b, t, panX)`:** a gradient plus 46 seeded thin streak lines (`rgba(170,195,230,.10)`, 2px) drifting right, longer near the viewer.
- **`reflect(horizon, fn, a)`:** clip to below the horizon, mirror with `translate(0, 2·horizon)` and `scale(1, -1)`, then redraw the far layers at alpha .25–.3 with `blur(3px)`. The boat reflection is squashed (`scale(1, -.6)`).
- **`haze(y, h, 'r,g,b', a)`:** a soft band where water meets sky. `vignette(a)`: a radial edge darkening plus a darker bottom 30%.
- **`tube(x1, y1, x2, y2, w, col)`:** a round-capped thick stroke. **All limbs, oars and poles are tubes**, which gives the puppet look for free.
- **`poly(points, col)`:** a flat polygon for torsos, rocks and fins.

### Puppet rig
- **`headProfile(x, y, s, o)`:** a profile head facing right.
  - Built from a skin ellipse (27×31), a nose triangle, an ear dot, a hair-cap path (`short`, or `long` with flowing hair), and an eye (white almond with a dark dot, or for `pale` ghosts a hollow socket with a glint), plus a mouth ellipse whose height is the `mouth` value (0–1, used for singing).
- **`rower(x, y, s, phase, stop, look)`:** a seated rower.
  - Lean is `.12·sin(phase)`.
  - The far arm (darker colours) is drawn behind the torso and the near arm in front. Each arm is an upper and lower tube to a hand circle.
  - The oar is a tube with a blade ellipse.
  - `stop` (0–1) blends the stroke to a halt, and `look` tilts the head up.
- **`siren(x, y, s, t, sing)`:**
  - The body is mirrored to face left.
  - The tail is a teal curve with a fin that sways (`sin(1.3t)`), with a few faint scale arcs.
  - Pale torso and arms, long black hair swaying, and the mouth opens and closes while singing.
- **`boat(x, y, s, bob, lamp)`:** a two-tone hull curve with a gunwale line, and a lantern on a pole with a warm glow. It bobs with `sin(2.1t)·5`.
- **Colours used:**
  - Sailor: skin `#c4895c` (shade `#a8714a`), shirt `#2e3f78` / `#34478a`, far arm `#24315f`.
  - Hull: `#4b2f22` / `#3a2219`. Lantern: `#f6c36c` with glow `255,190,110`.
  - Siren: skin `#b9c3c9`, tail `#2f6f73` / `#3f8d8f`.
  - Night sky: `#08101f` to `#13203a` to `#22314f`. Water: `#15233a` to `#05080f`.
  - Underwater: `#123044` to `#03070c`.

### Captions and title (as built)
- **Captions:**
  - Poppins 600 at 64px, white, with the active word in `#f2a65a`.
  - Shadow: `rgba(0,0,0,.45)`, blur 10, y 2.
  - Each chunk fades in and out over 0.15s. The active word is the last word whose start time has passed.
  - Placement is y = 370 (top) in most shots, or y = 1520 (lower) when the title card sits in the middle.
- **Title card:**
  - "FOLK HORROR TALES" in Poppins 600 at 26px, letter-spacing 9px, between two thin 110px rules.
  - The name in **Liberation Serif** at 128px, letter-spacing 10px, colour `#e9dcbc`.
  - "– Tale No. N –" in italic serif at 40px.
  - Fades in from 0.3s and out by about 3.4s.
- **Fonts:** Poppins from `fonts/` (woff2, loaded with `FontFace` before `READY`). The serif comes from the system Liberation Serif.

### Lessons
- Keep captions clear of the main subject (at first they overlapped the boat in the wide shot, so I moved them lower).
- Underwater scenes: draw the subject, then a translucent water veil (`rgba(20,48,66,.35)`), then the surface line and ripple ellipses, so things look submerged.
- Every scene stays readable with only 3–5 flat layers plus glows. Resist adding detail.
